MyHero — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

1. Who we are

MyHero ("the app") is a personal health-record app operated by BEST MEDIC SERVICES SDN. BHD. (Registration No. 202401034573 (1580420-U)) ("we", "us"). We are the party responsible for the personal data you enter into the app (the "data controller" under the Personal Data Protection Act 2010).

If you have any question about this policy or your data, contact us at support@myhero.care.

2. What this app is for

MyHero lets you keep your own health information in one place — "the page you can hand to any doctor". You enter your visits, medication, test results and other records; the app organises them so you (or a doctor you show them to) can understand your health at a glance. You may also keep records for family members you care for, with their permission.

3. What data we collect

We only collect what you choose to put into the app. That includes:

  • Account details: your name, email address, and (if you provide it) your identity-card (IC) number, which is used to work out your date of birth.
  • Health information you enter: medical conditions, medications, allergies, blood group, clinic and hospital visits, notes on what a doctor said, test and vital readings (such as blood pressure, blood sugar, HbA1c, cholesterol and weight), vaccinations, hospital admissions, and your surgical, family and obstetric history.
  • Documents you attach: photos or PDF files of medical reports, prescriptions and similar documents.
  • Visit recordings you make: when you use the "record what the doctor says" feature, your phone turns the speech into text on the device itself. The audio is never uploaded — only the text that you review and keep is saved.
  • Family profiles: if you keep a record for someone you care for, the information above about that person, together with a record that you confirmed you have their permission and when.

We do not collect analytics, advertising identifiers, your location, your contacts, or any tracking data. The app contains no advertising or third-party tracking tools.

4. Why we use it and our legal basis

We use your data only to provide the app to you: to store your records, show them back to you, organise them across the timeline, summary and trackers, and let you share them with a doctor. Our legal basis is your consent, which you give by creating an account and entering your data. Because health data is sensitive personal data under the PDPA, we treat it with extra care and ask for explicit consent before keeping records for a family member.

5. Where your data is stored

Your data is stored in a Microsoft Azure database located in the Malaysia West region — that is, it stays in Malaysia. Access is controlled by our servers, which require you to be signed in and to be a member of a record before it can be read or changed.

6. Who we share it with

We do not sell your data and we do not share it for advertising. We use two service providers who process data only on our behalf, under contract:

  • Microsoft Azure — hosts the database and the app's backend, in Malaysia.
  • Anthropic — when you scan a document or record a visit, the image or the text transcript is sent to Anthropic's AI service to help pull out details (such as a medicine name or a reading) for you to confirm. It is used only to process that request and is not used to train their models.

Beyond providing the app, we will only disclose your data if the law requires it.

7. AI features

The app can read a photo of a document or a visit transcript and suggest structured details (medication, readings, follow-up dates). This is decision support only — nothing is saved to your record until you review and confirm it. The app will not invent medical information: if the AI cannot be reached, only what you entered yourself is kept.

8. How long we keep it

We keep your data for as long as your account exists. When you delete your account (see section 10), we delete your account and the health data that belongs to you.

9. Your rights

Under the PDPA you may:

  • Access your data — the app lets you export everything from Settings → Export my data.
  • Correct your data — you can edit any record in the app at any time.
  • Delete your data — see section 10.
  • Withdraw consent — by deleting your account you withdraw your consent and remove your data.

10. Deleting your account and data

You can delete your account at any time from Settings → Delete my account. This permanently removes your account and the health records that belong to you.

If a record is shared with family members, deleting your account removes your access to it, but does not delete the record for the others who co-own it.

You can also request deletion by emailing support@myhero.care. Full instructions are here: https://myhero.care/delete-account.

11. Children

MyHero is not aimed at children. A parent or guardian may keep a family profile for a child they care for, and is responsible for that child's information.

12. Security

We protect your data with encryption in transit (HTTPS/TLS), signed-in access only, access control that checks your membership of a record on every request, and hashed passwords. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect sensitive health data.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will change the "last updated" date above and, for significant changes, tell you in the app.

14. Contact

Questions or requests about your data: support@myhero.care.
