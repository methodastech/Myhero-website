Deleting your MyHero account and data

You can delete a single record, or your whole account, at any time.

Deleting individual records — your account stays

You do not need to delete your account to remove something from it. Open the item in the app and tap the trash icon:

  • Visits — Visits tab, open the visit, then delete it.
  • Medicines — Records → Medications.
  • Test results, reports and imaging — Records → Test Reports.
  • Vaccinations — Records → Vaccinations.
  • Surgeries, admissions, family history and pregnancies — Records → Medical history.
  • Appointments — Visits → Appointments.
  • Attached photos and documents — open the Health Summary and tap the trash icon on the attachment.

The item is deleted from your account and from our servers. Everything else in your record is left untouched, and your account stays open.

1. In the app (fastest)

  1. Open MyHero and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile → Settings.
  3. Scroll to Danger zone and tap Delete my account.
  4. Type DELETE to confirm, then tap Delete permanently.

Your account and the health records that belong to you are removed straight away, and you are signed out.

2. By email

If you can't open the app, email support@myhero.care from the address on your account and ask us to delete your account. We will verify it is you and delete it.

What gets deleted

  • Your account (name, email, IC/date of birth).
  • Your own health records: visits, medication, allergies, conditions, test and vital readings, vaccinations, documents you attached, and the notes from any visit recordings.
  • Any family profile that only you keep.

What is kept

  • Records you share with family members are not deleted — deleting your account removes your access, but the relatives who co-own the record keep theirs.
  • Anonymous audit entries (who changed a shared record and when) may be kept without your name attached, to protect the other people who share that record.

How long it takes

Deletion from the app is immediate. Email requests are actioned within 7 days.
