2. What this policy covers

This policy covers the website at myhero.care and the MyHero app for Android and iOS. It applies to patients, family members and carers who use MyHero, and to clinics and their staff who contact us.

3. What we collect

On this website

  • Early access requests: your email address, when you ask for an invite.
  • Clinic enquiries: the clinic's name, your name, your phone number or email address, and anything you choose to write in the message.
  • Emails you send us: your email address and what you write.
  • Technical data: when you load a page, our hosting provider and Google Fonts receive your device's IP address and browser details, as any website does. We do not use this to identify you.

In the MyHero app

  • Account details: such as your name, email address or phone number, and date of birth.
  • Your health record: the reports, prescriptions, discharge notes and other documents you photograph or add; the values read from them; your readings such as blood pressure, blood sugar and weight; your medicines and their schedule; and your visits.
  • Sharing activity: who you share your record with, when, and when that share ends.

Information about your physical or mental health is sensitive personal data under the PDPA. We only process it with your explicit consent, and only to run MyHero for you.

4. Why we use it

  • To send your early access invite and tell you when MyHero is ready for you.
  • To reply to clinics and arrange for them to join.
  • To keep, organise and explain your health record in the app, and to prepare the one-page summary for your doctor.
  • To share your record with the people you choose, for as long as you choose.
  • To keep MyHero working and secure, fix problems and improve it.
  • To meet our legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal data, we do not trade it for advertising, and we do not use your health record to market anything to you. MyHero explains your record; it does not diagnose, and it does not replace your doctor.

5. Your consent

By sending us a form or using the app, you agree to us processing your personal data as this policy describes. For your health record, we ask for your explicit consent in the app before you add anything. You can withdraw your consent at any time by writing to us. If you do, we will stop processing that data, although we may then be unable to provide some or all of MyHero to you.

Giving us your email address or contact details on this website is voluntary. Without them, we cannot send your invite or reply to your enquiry.

6. Who we share it with

  • The people you choose: every share of your record is one you start, to one person, such as your doctor, and you can end it at any time.
  • Service providers: companies that host our website and app or deliver our emails, only to do that work for us, under contract and under our instructions.
  • When the law requires it: to a court, regulator or authority where Malaysian law requires us to.

We never share your health record with employers, insurers or advertisers.

7. Where it is kept

Your health record in the MyHero app is stored in Malaysia. Some of our service providers, such as the hosting for this website and Google Fonts, may process technical data outside Malaysia. Where personal data leaves Malaysia, we make sure it stays protected to the standard the PDPA requires.

8. How long we keep it

We keep personal data only as long as we need it for the purpose it was given:

  • Early access requests: until your invite has been sent, or until you ask us to remove you.
  • Clinic enquiries and emails: as long as we need to reply and follow up.
  • Your health record: for as long as you keep your MyHero account. If you delete your record or your account, we delete it, except for anything the law requires us to keep.

9. How we protect it

We take practical security steps to protect your personal data from loss, misuse and unauthorised access, including encrypted connections, restricted staff access, and service providers held to the same standard. If a breach of personal data happens, we will notify the Personal Data Protection Commissioner and, where it may cause you significant harm, tell you directly, as the PDPA requires.

10. Your rights

Under the PDPA, you can:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correct personal data that is inaccurate, incomplete or out of date.
  • Take your data with you: export your whole record as files you can keep, or ask us to send it to another service.
  • Delete your record or your account.
  • Withdraw consent, or ask us to limit how we process your data.
  • Stop direct marketing at any time. We will never send you marketing unless you have agreed to it.

To use any of these rights, write to hello@myhero.my. We may ask you to confirm who you are first. We will reply within 21 days, as the PDPA requires. If you are not satisfied with our answer, you may complain to the Personal Data Protection Commissioner of Malaysia.

11. Cookies and tracking

This website does not use cookies, analytics or advertising trackers. The page loads its fonts from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address to deliver them.

12. Children

MyHero accounts are for adults. A parent or legal guardian may keep a record for a child under 18 and consents on the child's behalf.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as MyHero grows. We will post the new version on this page with its effective date and, where the change is significant, tell you in the app or by email before it takes effect.
